How a listing earns the badge

No badge, no listing. Each step happens before a property is visible to buyers.

  1. Confirm the owner

    Identity and phone checks, plus a search for the same flat posted by other agents.

  2. Check the papers

    Ownership, approvals and RERA details are reviewed and any gaps are flagged on the listing.

  3. Visit and photograph

    Our team confirms the flat exists and photos are recent and match what you will see.

  4. Check availability weekly

    Listings that are sold or rented come down. Stale ones lose the badge.
